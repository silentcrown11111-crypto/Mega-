About Mega Sweets & Bakers

A family food house built on taste, trusted for generations.

Mega Sweets & Bakers began as a neighbourhood sweet shop and grew into a complete food destination. Today our kitchens run from dawn to midnight: bakers pulling the first loaves at six, halwais finishing trays of barfi by mid-morning, pizza ovens and grills firing through the evening, and a cash-and-carry floor stocked for the whole family.

One roof. One standard. Everything made with real ingredients, real time and the kind of care that makes customers come back for every occasion — from a Tuesday tea to a wedding of five hundred.

Made fresh, daily

Nothing sits overnight. Sweets, bread and pastries are made in-house every morning.

Bakery + restaurant

A full bakery, a mithai counter and a sit-down kitchen under one roof.

Every occasion

Eid, weddings, birthdays, office orders and quiet weeknight dinners.

Family friendly

Spacious dine-in, quick takeaway and a cash-and-carry floor for the weekly shop.

01The Mithai Counter

01 — Sweets

Sweets made the slow way.

Khoya reduced over hours, cashews ground fresh for kaju katli, syrup pulled to the exact thread. Our halwais still work by hand, tray by tray, every single morning.

  • Over 40 varieties of mithai, including sugar-free options
  • Gift boxes for Eid, weddings and corporate orders
  • Weighed fresh at the counter or pre-boxed to go

02 — Cakes & pastries

Celebrations built in layers.

From a half-kilo chocolate truffle to five-tier wedding towers, every cake is baked, filled and finished in our own studio. Pastries, cupcakes, brownies and donuts fill the counter beside them.

  • Custom cakes designed with you, ready in 48 hours
  • Eggless and reduced-sugar recipes available
  • Fresh cream, Belgian chocolate, real fruit

03 — Bakery

The ovens never really sleep.

Our bakers start at six. By the time the doors open, the shelves are stacked with sourdough, soft milk bread, buns, rusks, cookies and biscuits that smell like home.

  • Breads, buns and rusks baked twice daily
  • Cookies and biscuits by the box
  • Wholesale supply for cafés, schools and events

04 — Restaurant & fast food

Then the grill takes over.

Wood-fired pizzas, stacked burgers, crispy fries, wings, wraps and thick shakes — served fast, served hot, served late. Dine in with the family or take the whole order home.

  • Family dine-in and quick takeaway counter
  • Pizza, burgers, snacks and beverages in one order
  • Open until late, seven days a week
